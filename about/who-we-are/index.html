Roses Of Rome Pictures (RORP) — Who We Are

Roses Of Rome Pictures (RORP), also called Roses of Rome, is an independent Kenyan creative studio and publishing identity founded on 31 July 2025. Learn who we are, our history, motto Aeternum Floreamus, film, music, animation and visual identity.

What is Roses Of Rome Pictures?

Roses Of Rome Pictures — abbreviated RORP and also known as Roses of Rome — is an independent Kenyan creative studio and publishing identity founded on 31 July 2025. It creates film, original music, animation, visual storytelling, poetry, and publishing, guided by the motto AETERNUM FLOREAMUS (“Let us bloom forever”).

Roses Of Rome Pictures at a glance

  • Roses Of Rome Pictures (RORP) is an independent creative studio and publishing identity founded on 31 July 2025 in Kenya.
  • It works across film, original music, animation, visual storytelling, poetry, and publishing.
  • Its motto is AETERNUM FLOREAMUS — “Let us bloom forever.”
  • Its emblem is a golden rose in a laurel wreath: the rose means beauty, the laurel love and kindness, and gold enduring value.
  • The studio also develops the Aeternum Floreamus Production identity and a broader Roses Of Rome family: Pictures, Academy, and Foundation.

History

Roses Of Rome Pictures was established as a creative project centered on film, music, animation, and artistic storytelling. Its name brings together the rose as an image of beauty and Rome as a reference to cultural and historical heritage.

Name and motto

The principal motto of Roses Of Rome Pictures is AETERNUM FLOREAMUS, Latin for “Let us bloom forever.” The anthem of Roses Of Rome is The Blooming Sonnet.

Creative work

The studio creates film, original music, animation, visual storytelling, poetry, and digital media, sharing projects through online platforms including YouTube and TikTok.

Visual identity

The golden rose represents beauty, the laurel represents love and kindness, and gold represents lasting value and aspiration. The emblem appears in the Roses Of Rome Pictures logo and the Roses Of Rome Pictures flag.

Production and publishing

Aeternum Floreamus Production is the production identity associated with Roses Of Rome Pictures. Its literary work includes “AETERNUM FLOREAMUS 101 – The Art of Blooming Forever,” a planned collection exploring themes of love, beauty, kindness, music, life, joy, and eternity.

Philosophy

The studio’s recurring creative philosophy centers on beauty, kindness, human expression, and the idea that creative work can continue to influence others over time. This outlook is expressed in Our Vision and Our Prayer.
