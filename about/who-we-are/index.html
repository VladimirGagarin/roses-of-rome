Roses Of Rome Pictures (RORP) is an independent creative studio and publishing identity associated with film, music, animation, and visual storytelling. Founded on 31 July 2025 in Kenya, its motto is AETERNUM FLOREAMUS — Let us bloom forever.

The studio describes itself as “Connoisseurs of Film, Music & Animation.”

History

Roses Of Rome Pictures was established as a creative project centered on film, music, animation, and artistic storytelling. Its name brings together the rose as an image of beauty and Rome as a reference to cultural and historical heritage.

Creative work

The studio creates film, original music, animation, visual storytelling, poetry, and digital media, sharing projects through online platforms including YouTube and TikTok.

Its official anthem, “The Blooming Sonnet”, carries the promise “Roses of Rome, let us bloom forever.”

Visual identity

The golden rose represents beauty, the laurel represents love and kindness, and gold represents lasting value and aspiration. The emblem appears in the official logo and official flag.

Production and publishing

Aeternum Floreamus Production is the production identity associated with Roses Of Rome Pictures. Its literary work includes “AETERNUM FLOREAMUS 101 – The Art of Blooming Forever,” a planned collection exploring themes of love, beauty, kindness, music, life, joy, and eternity.

Philosophy

The studio’s recurring creative philosophy centers on beauty, kindness, human expression, and the idea that creative work can continue to influence others over time. This outlook is expressed in Our Vision and Our Prayer.
