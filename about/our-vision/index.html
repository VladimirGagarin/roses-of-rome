Our Vision — Beauty, Love & Kindness | Roses Of Rome Pictures

“Our Vision” sets out the studio's creed: beauty is heirloom and fire, love conquers all, and kindness crowns us all. It is a vision of art that outlives the moment in which it is created.

Roses of Rome Vision | Rosa Di Roma Visione

We are the ones who carve light from the stone,
Who turn every street into galleries unknown.
We paint the world in colors yet untold,
We shape the marble to outlast the gold.
This is our creed: Beauty is heirloom and fire.

Let’s paint our hearts with colors unafraid,
Let the crayons speak where words have strayed.
Let the brush weave dreams in hues of hope,
Let the canvas cradle our future bright.
As Roses of Rome, let us bloom forever.

We are the ones whose hearts are unbound,
Who turn pain into light from the deepest wound.
We give joy like rivers that never run dry,
We pray love will find us beneath every sky.
This is our vow: Love conquers all.

We are the ones who kneel in the dust,
Who turn strangers to kin through unwavering trust.
We give not just alms, but the keys to our gates,
We pray compassion outlives all our fates.
This is our law: Kindness crowns us all.

Roses of Rome, our vision is ambitious,
It speaks in volumes when beauty feels precious.
It rekindles hearts of the weary and small,
It is a bonfire where stories warm all.
It is a dream of love without end,
With our emblem: Aeternum floreamus, we ascend.
