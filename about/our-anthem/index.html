Our Anthem — Blooming Sonnet | Roses Of Rome Pictures Anthem Lyrics & Video

The official anthem of Roses Of Rome Pictures is “The Blooming Sonnet” — a song of beauty, memory, and the eternal bloom of the studio. Its chorus carries the studio's promise: “Roses of Rome, let us bloom forever.”

Roses Of Rome Pictures Anthem | The Blooming Sonnet | With Official Flag

When seasons come, our hearts will bloom,
Every petal within shines bright and true,
Plenty will flourish deep in our hearts,
We will bear the mark of Roses of Rome.

Roses of Rome,
Let us bloom forever,
Roses of Rome,
May we always be with you, ever.

Though time may fade the colors we wear,
Our roots stand firm where glory finds its home,
Through every tear, joy, and whispered prayer —
We still remain the Roses of Rome.

So when we part, let memory sing,
A love immortal carved in stone,
With every breath, in every spring—
We still remain the Roses of Rome.
