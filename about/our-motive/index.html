Our Motive — The Creative Purpose of Roses Of Rome Pictures

“Our Motive” is the poetic statement of purpose of Roses Of Rome Pictures — blooming in film, in music, and in song while carrying beauty forward through every frame and every tone.

We are Roses of Rome,
Blooming in film, in music, in song,
With every frame, with every tone,
We carry the beauty, we carry it on.

Connoisseurs of stories untold,
Of visions in silver, of hearts made bold,
Through sound and motion, through dream and flame,
We whisper of wonder, we honor our name.

Roses of Rome, forever we sing,
Film, music, animation — our offering.
Like petals eternal, our art shall remain,
In beauty, in memory, in joy, and in pain.

Roses of Rome, Roses of Rome,
Connoisseurs of art, this world is our home.
Film, music, animation — together we rise,
Blooming forever, beneath timeless skies.
