Our Prayer — The Roses Of Rome Pictures Prayer & Poem

“Our Prayer” is the Roses Of Rome Pictures poem of hope, love, kindness, and beauty — a prayer that what is beautiful inside us will never die, and that we will still remain the Roses of Rome.

We’ve seen the world rush on its way,
We’ve watched the joy turn shades of gray.
But in the soil from which we grow,
Lies a truth that all souls know.

Our Prayer, is in the heart that lives,
That love remains, forever it will stay.
Kindness is a beacon in the dark, you will see,
And the beauty inside, it will never die.
Our prayer remains, it will not pass by.

We send this wish on roots of hope,
A gentle, resilient, timeless scope.
Our hope is not a distant dream,
But light reflected in the stream.

So when we part, let memory sing,
A love immortal carved in stone.
With every breath, in every spring,
We still remain the Roses of Rome.
