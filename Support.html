Donate via M-Pesa

M-Pesa is a secure mobile money service widely used in Kenya for sending and receiving money. Even if you are abroad, you may still donate if your carrier or bank supports international transfers to M-Pesa.

A simple, secure gift

We are currently located in Kenya, Nairobi, and M-Pesa is a popular mobile payment method here. Learn more about M-Pesa.

Send to +254 741 146 031
  1. Go to M-Pesa menu on your phone.
  2. Select Lipa na M-Pesa.
  3. Choose Send Money.
  4. Enter number: +254 741 146 031.
  5. Enter the amount and confirm.

If you are outside Kenya, you can still donate through international partners that support M-Pesa, such as WorldRemit, Western Union, or banks that allow transfers to M-Pesa.
